Privacy Policy

huckleberry-sync is a personal, single-user tool operated by James Anderson for his own use only. It is not offered to the public.

It accesses the owner's own Google account (Gmail read-only, Drive files it creates, and Calendar events) solely to organize the owner's own data. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is stored only in the owner's own Google account.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
